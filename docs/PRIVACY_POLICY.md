## 2. Information we collect

### From customers (Customer App)

| Data | Purpose | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Full name, mobile number, email (optional), address | Identity, loan account, delivery of receipts/notices | Provided at onboarding |
| KYC document type + a masked identifier (e.g. last 4 digits) + a reference to a securely stored document image | Regulatory identity verification before extending credit | Provided during KYC submission |
| PIN (stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text) | App login | Set by the customer |
| Loan, EMI schedule, payment, and receipt records | Providing the core financing service | Generated by the service |
| Device identifier, platform, app version, push notification token | Sending EMI reminders/receipts, detecting a new/untrusted device at login | Collected automatically by the app |
| IP address, timestamps on OTP/login attempts | Fraud/abuse prevention, rate limiting | Collected automatically |
| Support ticket messages | Resolving customer support requests | Provided by the customer |

### From staff (Business App)

| Data | Purpose | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Name, mobile number, role, branch assignment | Staff account, access control | Provisioned by the business |
| Password (hashed), device identifier, push token | Staff login, step-up device verification, notifications | Set by staff / collected automatically |
| Actions taken in the app (loan approvals, payments collected, etc.) | Audit trail required for a financial ledger | Generated by the service |

We do **not** collect precise location, contacts, SMS/call logs, photos/media
beyond the specific KYC document upload flow, or any data unrelated to the
financing service.

## 3. How we use information

- To create and service loan accounts: origination, EMI scheduling, payment
  collection, receipts, and collections/reminders.
- To verify identity (KYC) as required by [FILL IN: applicable regulation,
  e.g. RBI/NBFC KYC norms].
- To secure accounts: OTP verification, device recognition, rate limiting
  against brute-force/abuse.
- To send transactional notifications (EMI due, payment received, receipt
  ready, support replies) via push notification and/or SMS.
- To process online EMI payments through our payment gateway provider.
- To respond to support requests.
- To comply with legal, regulatory, and audit requirements applicable to
  consumer lending.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for third-party
advertising.

plain text. All traffic between the apps and the backend is encrypted
(HTTPS/TLS). Access to customer and staff data is role-based and every
sensitive action is recorded in an append-only audit log. KYC documents are
referenced by pointer, not embedded in general application data, and stored
in [FILL IN: your document storage provider and its encryption-at-rest
